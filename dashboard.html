Why 98% reads high: the first 100 rows are naturally positive-skewed — this batch’s rating mix is 91★5, 4★1, 2★3, 2★4, 1★2. Reviews at the extremes (★1 / ★5) agree with the model almost perfectly; disagreements concentrate in the mid ratings, so a batch with more mixed reviews scores lower. Note too that a few “misses” are cases where the text disagrees with the star rating — the model can only judge the words.
